# Homebody Mission Control — strategy

> Status: starting slate, 2026-09-24. Confirm at the first huddle.

## What this program is

A twice-weekly war room for Homebody, including Homebody Guaranty (HBG). It exists because Summit customer feedback and live client heat were moving across Product, Engineering, Customer Success, and Insurance Ops faster than a status meeting can hold.

HBG is a Homebody product. It is a lane on this surface, not a second Mission Control.

## What success looks like in the first two weeks

Every hot client thread has:

- a track
- a named owner
- a next step that can happen before the following huddle
- a source (Slack channel, ticket, or meeting)

Program-level adoption and revenue metrics stay on the Homebody Quarter Brief until this war room chooses its own scorecard. No numeric target is set here yet.

## Lanes

### HBG

Homebody Guaranty: renewals, eligibility, transfers, client go-lives, and enrollment exceptions. Insurance Ops and Product share this lane.

### Homebody

Everything else the war room is carrying: the Homebody app, migration heat, and resident-experience issues that are not HBG. Owners add these tracks. The intake card in `src/data/tracks.json` is a placeholder.

## Cadence

- Twice weekly huddle, run against this surface.
- Continuous: update your track when the fact changes, not at the meeting.
- Working rule: if it is not in Mission Control, it did not happen.
